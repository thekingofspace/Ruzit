Donate

Ruzit is free for anything you build, commercial or not, with no license fee. The only thing it ever asks is a credit. If the engine saved you time or money, a donation helps keep development going, but it is entirely optional and nothing is gated behind it.

Support Ruzit

Donations go directly toward development time, hardware for testing, and the third-party tools and services the engine depends on. One-off tips and recurring support are both welcome and any amount is appreciated.

If the widget above does not load, you can open the page directly: ko-fi.com/raikin77.

You don't have to pay to use Ruzit. There is no paid tier, no locked features, and no trial. Donating is purely a way to say thanks and help the project keep moving. See the license for the one requirement: crediting Ruzit somewhere in your project.

Other ways to help

Not in a position to donate? These cost nothing and help just as much:

  • Star the project on GitHub.
  • Report bugs and suggest features through the issue tracker.
  • Tell other developers about Ruzit, or share what you've built with it.
  • Hang out and help others in the Discord.
  • Keep the "Made with Ruzit" credit visible in your shipped game.
